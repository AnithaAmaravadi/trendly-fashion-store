## ✨ Features

### 🛍️ Shopping Experience
- **20+ Trending Products:** High-quality images for Men & Women.
- **Smart Filtering:** Filter by All, Women, Men, Trending, and New Arrivals.
- **Live Search:** Instantly search for outfits as you type.
- **Product Details:** Click on any product to see full details, descriptions, and reviews.

### 💰 Indian E-Commerce Ready
- **Indian Currency:** All prices displayed in ₹ (Rupees) with proper formatting (e.g., ₹1,999).
- **GST Calculation:** Automatic 18% GST calculation at checkout.
- **Shipping Logic:** Free shipping on orders above ₹999, else flat ₹99.

### 🛒 Cart & Checkout
- **Shopping Cart:** Add, remove, and update quantities seamlessly.
- **Multiple Payment Options:** Credit/Debit Card, UPI, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- **Order History:** Track your past orders and current status.

### 👤 User & Account
- **Login / Register:** Secure demo authentication.
- **Address Management:** Save multiple Indian shipping addresses with PIN codes.
- **Dark/Light Mode:** Toggle theme from the side menu or settings.

### ⭐ Reviews & Ratings
- **Real Reviews:** See verified customer reviews for every product.
- **Write Reviews:** Logged-in users can rate products and write their own reviews.

### 📱 Fully Responsive
- Beautifully designed for Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop screens.
- **Side Navigation Menu:** Easy access to all pages on mobile devices.

---

## ️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (with CSS Variables for theming), Vanilla JavaScript
- **Icons:** Font Awesome 6
- **Data Persistence:** LocalStorage (Cart, User, Orders, Reviews)
- **Maps:** OpenStreetMap integration for Contact page
