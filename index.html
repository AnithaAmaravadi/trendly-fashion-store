How It All Started

Trendly began in a small Mumbai apartment in 2020 with a simple idea: fashion should be effortless, affordable, and fun.

What started as a passion project between three friends has grown into a community of over 2 lakh happy customers across 500+ cities in India.

Today, we curate the trendiest outfits for men and women, working directly with designers and manufacturers to bring you styles that are fresh, quality-checked, and priced fairly.

Every piece in our collection is handpicked. Every customer is family. That's the Trendly promise. 💖
